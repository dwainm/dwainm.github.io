The shape up book has been on my reading list for a very long time.

It's very prescriptive.

The betting table is not great for companies with a lot of moving parts. You have to maintain some kind of backlog of ideas. Not work for complex businesses.

The best part is the shaping time. Which is what the title suggest. Whe you decide to do something. Shape it first. Decidef what you'll do, what no, what rabit holes to avoid and then do just that.

The other best part is the cut work up into a cycle . Thei have 6 weeks. But make sure all work fits in that 6 weeks and ship it out. Even if an MVP, get it out.
