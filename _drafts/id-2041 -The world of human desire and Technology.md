Artificial Intelligence promise to solve many porblems, but it just like all other tech will still be subject to human greed and I'm writing this post to explore the thought. This has probably been said before, but we don't need better tools, we need bettered humans. I aslwo want to highlight how greed is the actualy problem in this world, not solutions to problems.

> **intelligence**
> 
> /ɪnˈtɛlɪdʒ(ə)ns/
> 
> The ability to acquire and apply knowledge and skills."an eminent man of great intelligence"

Knowledge of what? Of solving equations, playing games, having conversations?

Artificial Intelligent systems may replace some or even all of our current daily tasks. Will Artificial Intelligence cover the broader aspects of the human experience? I think

What about the satisfaction of doing something. It is totally understandable to give tasks that you don't enjoy to your robot counterpart, but what if you really enjoy a long drive through the country and doing the driving yourself? What if you love knitting and making jerseys by hand? In the world of black and white, there is no space fo joy and happiness. Theres is only efficient and inefficient processes. There are over simplifications of the human experience and there are deceptive levels of greed. There is an illusive thought that the system is good for all because it's good for the creator.

Family members ask me about AI

I’m a tech guy

I don’t know anything really but the little I know is this:

If A I destroys human life’s it will be at the edge of human will.

What desire does a compute have to take lives of not prompted by human desire?

How can we fool ourselves into thinking that we are merely flesh and bone. There is something in us our drive our will and our passion.

You can turn your robot on but you will never feel the warmth of its attempt to love you.

You can turn your smart card on but even the smartest of humans have succumbed to the feeling of uselessness.

You have the bots do all your work but suffer from the lack of feeling accomplished the lack of meaning and expression.

Let AI entertain you while you deeply crave the simplicity of human interaction.

We don’t desire to do, we desire to be. If doing comes from being we come full circle and feel heard, satisfied and at peace.

No robot will ever understand peace. Inner peace. The piece so deep that if disturbs threatens our existence.

AI will be to us as all the thigbgs we have created a mere tool... to use for common good or not.

If jobs are lost. Blame greed.

If blood is shed blame greed.

If chaos erruprts blame greed.

Blame the greedy fool who’s trying divert our attention to the tool. This tool is not smart. It just executed well.

Even the coolest technology can become outdated and uninteresting. If AI become so good at massages that we don’t need humans. A. New market will emerge as we crave those who are like us.

AI is not is. It is not like us. It will never be as we are. Human.

We are not in an AI world. AI is in a world shaped by human will and desire
