
Rails is closer to Ruby than laravel is to PHP. Thechnically , they'r both frameworks, but what seperates them is this: rails can bend ruby t do what it wants, Laravell has to work around PHP to do what it wants. Rails is Ruby, it feels like a version/implementation of ruby and  Laravel feels like a framework written in PHP, but with so many things in PHP, that the framework doesn't controll.

You can build most things in both frameworks. So, this is all a matter of preference. There are obvious things that one does better than the other and one community might be leading in areas where the othes isn't.
