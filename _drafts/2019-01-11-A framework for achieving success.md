The most impoastand take a way from this post is. Figure out what you want to go for nad start working.

I became obsessed with figuring out what success is and how to get there, but I soon realised that many who have gone ahead of me have nailed it down to a few disciplines and ways to think.. This made my quest very easy and short lived. I found the answer to all my questions and I realised that though there are only a dosen habits you should cultivate, it is a journey and a way of thinking, not a destination and a few easy steps.

I have not nailed this down. I have simply worked out a strategy that I think will take me from where I am to where I want to be. This may be of no value to you but I hope my method inspires you to search out your own framework for getting where you want to be. This is not me saying that I have made it. It is me saying. This is the direction in which I would like to go.

I may write again in future and say how totally wrong I was, but that doesn't matter. I would have learnt more in the process of reaching high than in the comfort of relaxing while time passes by.

Now, While looking hard for advice looking for the answer to how to really achiev succees. All my advise boils down to this paragraph:

Succuss starts in the mind, not in the strange weird sense but, in a way of thinking, a way of seeing the world a way of seeing yourself and improving those thoughts all the time, along with this is how you think of others. Thinking flows over into action, the right thinking will resuld in the right kind of action. The right kind of action should result in results, which will need to be re-evaluated. The action is part of the process and thoughts alone will not bring any results. This loops back and informs your thinking again and so the process continues. The way you think can be faster influenced by in this order: the people you spend most time with, the content you consume, and your willingness to be critical about your thoughts.  All of the above goes through a filter called vision or desire. What you desire to see your vission.

Success is stacking the right mix of skills coupled with experience in applying them in the context of a growing industry. It is placing yourelf in a bigger better pond, making sure you're not the biggest fish. It is long term planning and the context and awareness of evelating things with a long term mindset.
