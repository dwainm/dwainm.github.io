I've been interested in the stock market for a while now, but have always been on the sidelines looking in, until Lauren and I recently looked at investing on a monthly basis. Through some trial and error we've managed to make a few good trades, but this post is not about investing. As a software engineer I'm always fascinated by how things work. So, after placing a few trades I started wondering about the stock market and how it works.

The best way to learn how something works for me has always been to take things apart and put them together again, when thinking about a piece of software, it would always be to break it by introducing my own code and as a result I form basic understanding of the flow and why things are the way they are.
