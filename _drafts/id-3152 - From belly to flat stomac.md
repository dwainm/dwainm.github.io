1. Water: Drink 1 litre when you wake up and another 1.5 throughout the day. If you can get to three every day then you're doing well. Drink water before and after meals.

3. Sleep. Make sure you get enough sleep. Eveyryone needs different amount. I found that 9 hours is good for me and then I try to get extra hour on the weekend. Without enough sleep you'll need to be a pro athlete to look amazing.

5. Movement. Light Excercise: Walk at least 20 mins per day (make it part of your routine). Do as many pushups as you can every morning and do a full body stretch.

7. Fasting: Try to keep your eating in a window. Give your body enough time rest after your last meal. Having an eating window of 8-9 hours is a good target. Stay away from food from a set time every day. Mine is 7pm-10am.

9. Food. Try to stick to a low carb diet. Mostly keep away from sugar/chocoloatse sweets. Eat enough to feel full. Enjoy your foods and have fun at parties, but then make sure your regular routine.
