---
title: "Instant Pot Dal"
---

**Plant-based | Instant Pot | One-pot | Make-ahead friendly**

A simple North Indian dal of red lentils and brown lentils made in the Instant Pot, letting the natural sweet flavour of masoor shine through with brown lentils adding bite and body. Minimal spices, maximum taste — pantry staples and one pot from start to finish.

---

## Prep — Soak the Dal

1. Add masoor dal and brown lentils to a large bowl.
1. Wash 3-4 times until the water runs clear.
1. Cover with fresh water and soak for **1 hour**.
1. Drain before cooking.

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
|------------|--------|-------|
| Masoor dal (red lentils) | 3 cups (570g) | |
| Brown lentils | 1 cup (190g) | Hold their shape — add bite to the dal |
| Tomatoes | 2 × 400g cans peeled tomatoes | Drain nothing, tip the whole can in |
| Green chilies, slit | 5-6 | Optional, adjust to heat preference |
| Water | 7 cups | Ratio 7 cups water to 4 cups dry dal |
| Salted stock blocks | 3½ blocks | 1 block per 2 cups water. Dissolve in the cooking water. |
| **For the Tadka** | | |
| Ghee | 8-9 tbsp | Or 5 tbsp oil / 10 tbsp butter |
| Dried red chilies, broken | 8-9 | |
| Garlic cloves, chopped | 15-17 | |
| Mustard seeds | 1¼ tsp | Optional |
| Cumin seeds | 2½-3 tsp | |
| Hing (asafoetida) | ¾ tsp | Optional but recommended |
| Salt | 3-3½ tsp | Adjust to taste |
| Kashmiri red chili powder | 1½-2½ tsp | Use 1½ for mild, up to 2½ for heat |
| Turmeric | 1¼ tsp | |
| Garam masala | 1 tsp | Add more if you want |
| Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves) | 2½-3 tsp | Crushed between palms |
| **To Serve** | | |
| Fresh coriander leaves, chopped | 10-11 tbsp | |
| Lemon juice | 7-8 tbsp | Fresh squeezed |

---

## Method

1. Get all ingredients ready.
1. **Sauté the tadka**: Press Sauté on the Instant Pot and heat the ghee (or oil/butter). Add mustard seeds, cumin seeds, dried red chilies, and chopped garlic. Sauté until the garlic turns lightly golden.
1. **Bloom the spices**: Add the tomatoes, salt, chili powder, hing, garam masala, and turmeric. Sauté 1 minute.
1. **Add the dal**: Add the soaked and drained dal, green chilies, and 7 cups water with 3½ stock blocks dissolved in it. Stir well and deglaze the pot.
1. **Pressure cook**: Secure the lid and set the steam release to Sealing. Pressure cook on High for 12 minutes.
1. **Release**: Let the pressure release naturally (10-15 min). The red lentils will have melted into the sauce and the brown lentils will still hold their shape. Open and stir in the crushed kasuri methi.
1. **Finish**: Taste and adjust salt or garam masala. If too runny, sauté a few minutes to thicken; if too thick, add a splash of hot water.
1. Squeeze lemon juice and garnish with fresh coriander. Serve hot with basmati rice, roti, or naan.

---

## Variations

**Extra veg**: Add a handful of spinach, chopped carrots, or fresh methi leaves in the last 5 minutes of simmering.

**Richer**: Use 4 tbsp ghee in the tadka and finish with a splash of cream or coconut milk.

**Smokier**: Use the dhungar method — place a red-hot charcoal piece in a small steel bowl inside the dal pot, pour 1 tsp ghee over it, cover immediately and let smoke infuse for 5 minutes.

---

## Batch Cooking

- **Storage**: Keeps 5 days refrigerated, 3 months frozen.
- **Improves overnight**: Flavours deepen — great for meal prep.
- **Reheats**: Stovetop or microwave, add a splash of water if thickened.
