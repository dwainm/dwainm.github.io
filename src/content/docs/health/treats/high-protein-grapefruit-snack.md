**Plant-based | No-cook | 5 minutes | Snack**

A fast grapefruit snack inspired by this short: [High Protein Grapefruit Breakfast](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/gahyqGm5erI). Creamy soy yogurt plus seeds turns citrus into a filling, protein-forward bowl.

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
|------------|--------|-------|
| Grapefruit | 1 | Pink or ruby, peeled and segmented |
| Unsweetened soy yogurt | 3/4 cup | High-protein base |
| Hemp seeds | 1 tbsp | |
| Chia seeds | 1 tsp | Optional |
| Cinnamon | Pinch | Optional |
| Maple syrup | 1 tsp | Optional, to taste |

---

## Method

1. Get all ingredients ready.
1. **Prep grapefruit**: Peel and segment grapefruit, removing thick membranes if preferred.

1. **Build bowl**: Add soy yogurt to a bowl and top with grapefruit segments.

1. **Finish**: Sprinkle hemp seeds, chia, and cinnamon on top. Add maple syrup if you want it sweeter.

1. **Serve**: Eat immediately.

---

## Tips

**Protein boost**: Use the highest-protein unsweetened soy yogurt you can find

**Meal prep**: Keep grapefruit and yogurt separate, then assemble just before eating

---
