---
title: Applesauce
---

*Adapted from [Natasha's Kitchen](https://natashaskitchen.com/applesauce-recipe/)*

## Ingredients

- 10 lbs apples, very ripe (any variety)
- 1½ cups water
- 2 Tbsp sugar (optional, or to taste)
- Cinnamon (optional, for serving)

## Method

1. Get all ingredients ready.
1. **Prepare apples:** Peel, core, and slice all apples.
1. **Make sugar solution:** Add water and sugar to a large stockpot over medium heat. Bring to a boil to dissolve sugar.
1. **Cook:** Add sliced apples, reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for 1 hour, stirring occasionally until apples soften and shrink down.
1. **Blend:** Mash apples to your desired consistency using a potato masher, immersion blender, or electric hand mixer.
1. Serve once cooled, with cinnamon if desired.

## Storage

- **Refrigerate:** Up to 2 weeks in an airtight container
- **Freeze:** Up to 3 months in freezer-safe bags
- **Can:** Water bath process for up to 1 year shelf life
