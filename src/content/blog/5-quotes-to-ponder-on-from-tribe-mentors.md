I wrote a [quick review on the book](/tribe-of-mentors-quick-review/), A tribe of mentors, but wanted to go into more detail and give you some of the quotes that that really hit home for me. I chose 5 of them to share with you.

I truly found a lot of value in this book. If you are interested, go ahead and read my [quick review here]({% link _posts/2018-03-07-tribe-of-mentors-quick-review.md %}) and then get a copy for yourself.

<!--more-->

#### 1\. Perspective and focus:

> "It is not what you look at that matters, it is what you see."
> 
> [Henry David Thoreau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_David_Thoreau)

#### 2\. Good decisions:

> "Hard choices, easy life, easy choices hard life"  
> 
> [Jerzy Gregory](https://tim.blog/2017/03/16/jerzy-gregorek/)

#### 3\. Being true:

> "Integrity is the only path where you will never get lost".
> 
> [Mike Maples Jr.](https://floodgate.com/mike-maples-jr/)

#### 4\. Hanging around the right people:

> "It is the **quality** of your relationships that determine the **quality** of your life"
> 
> _[Esther Perel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esther_Perel)_

#### 5\. On taking action:

> "To avoid criticism, say nothing, do nothing, be nothing"
> 
> [Elbert Hubbard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbert_Hubbard)
