Today marks the start of a new and exciting journey. I’m starting in a new role as a Developer at [Woothemes](http://www.woothemes.com/).

For the past two years I’ve been working with an amazing team in the technical support department . I’ve learnt so much about our clients and their needs. The support team serves as the frontline for [Woothemes](http://www.woothemes.com/) .  I’m excited to apply my experience gained in my new role.

My main focus will be to Work on our [Sensei](http://www.woothemes.com/products/sensei/) ( Learner Management System ) plugin. I’ve been a contributor on [Sensei](http://www.woothemes.com/products/sensei/) for the past few months, working weekends and after hours. With more time to focus on what I love, I aim to improve Sensei’s functionality, code and developer documentation.

Finally with more time after hours I look forward to learning and improving my skills as a software engineer.
